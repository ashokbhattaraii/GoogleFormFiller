User Perception and Requirements for an AI-Powered Cloud-Based Public Notice Management System

This survey is conducted to collect user opinions and requirements for developing an AI-powered cloud-based public notice management platform for Nepal. The purpose of this survey is to understand the current challenges faced by citizens in accessing government notices and identify useful features such as centralized notice access, AI-based summaries, secure cloud storage, notifications, search functionality, and document-based question answering.

Your responses will be used only for academic research purposes and will remain confidential. The survey will take approximately 3–5 minutes to complete. Thank you for your participation.
